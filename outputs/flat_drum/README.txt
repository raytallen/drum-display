DRUM — JETBRAINS MONO

Open flat_digit_drum.3mf in Bambu Studio. Print four sets.
Assign separate colors to the core/backgrounds and digits. Keep each digit
and its background together as parts of the same tile. Glue the finished
tiles into the core in order, 0–9.

The drum has been printed successfully. It fits directly on the motor shaft.
The editable generator is make_flat_drum.py; supply --font with a
JetBrainsMono-Bold.ttf file. Dependencies are in the root requirements.txt.
