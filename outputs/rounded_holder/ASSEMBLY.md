# Holder

Open `complete_holder.3mf` in Bambu Studio. Print the housing upright with supports off and the motor plates flat, countersinks up. Select your printer and filament profiles.

Use six M3 × 3 mm heat-set inserts (CNC Kitchen TC-M3x3.0), six M3 × 6 panel screws, and sixteen M3 × 5, 90° countersunk motor screws with heads no larger than 6 mm.

Install inserts first. Attach motors to the plates with screw heads flush, then fit the drums 4.5 mm from the inward plate faces. Slide each complete side assembly in from the rear and secure its three screws.

Geometry and slicing checks passed; physical fit and assembly still need verification.
